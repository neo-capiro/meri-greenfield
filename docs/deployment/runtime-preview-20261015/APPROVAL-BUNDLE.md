## Cost decision

Current catalog arithmetic is in [pricing/pilot-recalculation.json](pricing/pilot-recalculation.json): **13.29USD for the bounded24h assumptions**. [Implementation review](IMPLEMENTATION-REVIEW.md) records the source-only packet and blockers. The following estimates and [cost-estimate.json](cost-estimate.json) are retained historical proposals, superseded by that fresh receipt. For the proposed reduced preview, expected incremental24h AWS cost is about **$10–15 including one build/ancillary reserve**; request **$25 planning reserve**, subject to current project accounting. This is not a hard invoice cap. Existing paid dedicated endpoints/DNS/storage continue charging separately. The same reduced services left running730h are roughly **$250–320/month incremental**, or **$300–450/month including an illustrative existing baseline**; ongoing operation is not approved.

The accepted HA Temporal topology's10 server replicas alone total12vCPU/24GiB using the fixture's provisional sizes, about **$433/month compute**; with control/poller/proxy/relay, Multi-AZ DB, endpoints and ancillary costs, plan **$750–1,000/month** pending an exact quote/load sizing. This is why the old unapproved $15 pilot reservation cannot silently become continuous hosting or HA acceptance.

Prices are USD on-demand US East estimates,730h/month, no free tier/credits/Savings Plans assumed. [Fargate pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/fargate/pricing/) supplies the published per-second example converted to hourly; [ALB pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/elasticloadbalancing/pricing/) supplies hourly+LCU costs; [VPC IPv4 pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/vpc/pricing/) and [PrivateLink pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/privatelink/pricing/) cover addresses/endpoints. DB rates are explicitly historical14 October shipping price receipts, subject to a fresh [RDS quote](https://aws.amazon.com/rds/postgresql/pricing/). [Secrets Manager](https://aws.amazon.com/secrets-manager/pricing/) charges and [KMS pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/kms/pricing/) are included in ancillary allowances. Price changes, cross-AZ/US model transfer, logs, retained state, burst credits or excess LCU/traffic can exceed estimates; alarms and stop/cleanup authority bound operational exposure, not billing mechanically.
