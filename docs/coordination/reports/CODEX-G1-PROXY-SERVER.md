Only one pump owns the actual ASGI receive callable. Its queue holds at most two events, plus at most one event held by that pump; aggregate request bytes/events are bounded. It receives disconnects and denies continuation. The upstream receives through the bounded channel. Response events are checked for start/body order, typed status/headers/body/more-body, and aggregate byte/event bounds. The watcher is independent of downstream `send`; the caller races upstream completion against deny and the original deadline. After response start, failure does not invent a successful terminal event or publish another error body.
